01 / Get to know the strand

What is the STEM strand?

STEM stands for Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics. In Senior High School, it is an academic strand that develops students' knowledge and skills in scientific ideas, mathematical reasoning, research, and problem-solving. It is a strong foundation for many science- and technology-related college programs.

🔬

Science

Explore living things, matter, energy, the environment, and how the natural world works through observation and experiments.

Discover & investigate
💻

Technology

Learn how digital tools, computer systems, and innovation can help people communicate and solve real-world problems.

Create & innovate

Engineering

Apply science and mathematics to design, test, and improve structures, machines, processes, and systems.

Design & build

Mathematics

Use numbers, patterns, equations, statistics, and logical reasoning to understand and solve problems.

02 / College possibilities

What courses can a STEM student take?

STEM can prepare you for a wide range of college programs. Your best choice depends on your interests, strengths, career goals, and the admission requirements of each college or university.

🩺

Health & Medical Sciences

BS Nursing, BS Medical Technology / Medical Laboratory Science, BS Pharmacy, BS Physical Therapy, BS Occupational Therapy, BS Biology, and Doctor of Medicine (after a bachelor's degree and required admission).

🛠️

Engineering & Architecture

BS Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics, Chemical, Computer, Industrial, or Agricultural Engineering; BS Architecture.

🖥️

Computing & Technology

BS Computer Science, BS Information Technology, BS Information Systems, BS Data Science, and other computing-related programs.

🌿

Natural & Environmental Sciences

BS Chemistry, BS Physics, BS Mathematics, BS Statistics, BS Environmental Science, BS Geology, BS Meteorology, and BS Marine Biology.

🌾

Agriculture & Food Sciences

BS Agriculture, BS Forestry, BS Fisheries, BS Food Technology, BS Nutrition and Dietetics, and related programs.

✈️

Other STEM-related fields

BS Aviation-related programs, BS Mathematics Education, science education, and specialized applied-science programs, depending on the institution.

Important: Being a STEM student does not automatically guarantee admission to a particular course. Many colleges accept applicants from different SHS strands, while some programs may have prerequisite subjects, entrance exams, grade requirements, or bridging courses. Always check the school's current admission rules.

🏗️

👩‍💻

04 / Choose with confidence

How to choose your college course

You do not need to decide your entire future right away. Start by exploring and comparing.

Know your interests

Which subjects and activities do you enjoy—biology, math, computers, designing, or helping people?

Research programs

Read course descriptions, subject lists, possible careers, costs, scholarships, and admission requirements.

Compare your options

Talk with teachers, guidance counselors, students, and professionals. Make a practical shortlist.
